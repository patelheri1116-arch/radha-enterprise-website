Who We Are

Your Trusted Steel Partner in Rajkot

Established in 2004 in Samrat Industrial Area, Rajkot, Radha Enterprise has evolved into one of the region's most reliable stockists and suppliers of premium stainless steel round bars and industrial steel products.

We pride ourselves on our uncompromising quality standards, transparent dealings, and quick order turnarounds. Whatever grade or diameter you need for your engineering and manufacturing applications, Radha Enterprise delivers precision-tested steel tailored to your requirements.

Our Mission

To deliver top-grade steel products with maximum reliability, competitive market pricing, and swift order fulfillment.

Our Vision

To remain the premier and most trusted steel stockist in Rajkot and surrounding industrial belts.

Infrastructure & Stocking

Warehouse & Stockyard

Extensive Grade Range

Complete ready stock of SS 304, 304L, 316, 316L, 410, 420, and 431 round bars in standard mill lengths.

Custom Cutting Services

Equipped with heavy-duty bandsaw machinery to supply custom cut-to-length bars tailored to your specifications.

Precision Processing

Custom Cut-to-Length

Need specific sizes without purchasing full mill lengths? We operate heavy-duty bandsaw cutting machines capable of delivering millimeter-precise lengths tailored to your engineering requirements.

Every cut piece is thoroughly deburred, quality-checked, and securely bundled—ready for immediate pickup or direct delivery to your workshop floor.

Millimeter Accuracy

Clean, square bandsaw cuts with tight length tolerances to reduce material wastage and machining prep time.

Ready for Immediate Handover

Cut pieces are tagged, strapped, and staged in our loading area for instant customer dispatch.

Local Dispatch & Transport

Fast Regional Transport

Located centrally in Samrat Industrial Area, Rajkot, we ensure smooth transport coordination across Rajkot and neighboring industrial hubs like Shapar, Metoda, Ribda, Aji GIDC, etc.

Priority Dispatch Commitment: Once your order is confirmed, our team prioritizes immediate material handling and dispatch—aiming to deliver your order as early as possible, to keep your production moving without delay.
